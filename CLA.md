This agreement covers every **Contribution** you submit to any ezCORE
repository: the app, the runtime, the core API, and their documentation
and tests.

## 1. Definitions

- **"You"**: the individual, or the legal entity on whose behalf you act, that
  submits a Contribution.
- **"Maintainer"**: the person who controls the GitHub account `0xJ1nn`, and
  any successor to whom this agreement is assigned under §8 (for example, a
  company or foundation that the Maintainer forms to run ezCORE).
- **"Contribution"**: any original work of authorship, including code,
  documentation, tests and assets, that You intentionally submit to an ezCORE
  repository. It includes modifications to existing work, and covers any
  channel: pull requests, patches, issues and comments containing code.
- **"Project Licence"**: the licence stated in the `LICENSE` file of the
  repository You contribute to, at the moment You contribute.

## 5. What You confirm

You confirm that:

1. **You have the right.** The Contribution is your original work, or You

3. **No circumvention.** Your Contribution contains no keys, decryption

4. **AI assistance is disclosed and reviewed.** If an AI tool generated any

5. **You will tell us.** You will inform the Maintainer if any of the above

## How to sign

Comment on your first pull request with:

> I have read the ezCORE CLA v1.0 and I agree to its terms.

Then sign off every commit (`git commit -s`), as required by the Developer
Certificate of Origin. The maintainer's own commits are covered by the
maintainer's ownership; no signature is needed.
