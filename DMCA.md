# DMCA policy

This project ships no copyrighted game content, BIOS/firmware, keys, or
cheat databases, and links to none. If you believe any file in this
repository or its releases infringes your rights:

- **Fastest path:** GitHub's copyright claim form —
  <https://github.com/contact/dmca> — which routes the notice to this
  repository and its maintainer.
- You can also open an issue titled `DMCA notice` (without the material
  itself) to establish contact; a private channel will be arranged.

Response commitments:

- **Acknowledge** a complete notice within **48 hours**.
- **Remove or disable access** to the identified material while it is
  evaluated, and **notify the contributor** who added it.
- **Counter-notices** follow 17 U.S.C. § 512(g); material is restored per
  the statutory process when a valid counter-notice applies.

Repeat-infringer policy: contributors who submit infringing content after
warning are blocked from the repository.

ROM-request issues are closed with a pointer to this file and
[`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).
