# DMCA policy

This project ships no copyrighted game content, BIOS/firmware, keys, or
cheat databases, and links to none. If you believe any file in this
repository or its releases infringes your rights:

- Contact the DMCA agent listed in GitHub's repository settings
  (to be appointed before v0.1.0 public release).
- Takedown SLA: acknowledge in 48h, remove or disable access to the
  identified material, notify the contributor.
- Counter-notices follow 17 U.S.C. § 512(g).

Repeat-infringer policy: contributors who submit infringing content after
warning are blocked. ROM-request issues are closed by bot with a pointer
to this file.
