## ezCORE Diamond

Diamond is an optional subscription in the App Store and Google Play builds.
It supports the project and unlocks nothing. Every feature, every core and
every platform are free for everyone, always, whether or not a person
subscribes. A member gets:

- the Diamond badge and accent in the app;
- the option to have their name included in the credits.

Membership changes nothing about the software. New features and cores reach
everyone at the same time.

### Subscription, renewal and cancellation

Diamond is offered as a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription. Payment is
handled by Apple or Google. The subscription renews automatically at the end
of each period unless you cancel it before the renewal date.

- **App Store:** open Settings, tap your name, open Subscriptions, choose
  ezCORE and select Cancel Subscription.
- **Google Play:** open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, open Payments
  and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose ezCORE Diamond and select
  Cancel subscription.

Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not end a period you have already
paid for. The price and renewal date for your region are shown by the store
before you confirm a purchase.

If a free trial is offered, its length and conditions are shown by the store
before you start it. When the trial ends the subscription begins and renews as
described above unless you cancel before the trial is over.

Refunds for a subscription are handled by the store under its own refund
policy. Contact Apple or Google, not the project, for a refund request.
