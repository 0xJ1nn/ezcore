# Privacy

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

ezCORE runs on your device. There is no account, no server and no data
collection. This page describes the small amount of information the app keeps
locally and the two cases where a request leaves the device.

## What is stored on your device

Everything ezCORE keeps lives in the app's own folder on the device you use.
Nothing is uploaded.

- **Library index** — the games you add, their location, the core you chose for
  each one, your favourites and how you like the library sorted.
- **Saves** — save states and battery-save data, kept with the games they
  belong to.
- **Settings** — appearance, controls, audio and the other local preferences
  described in Settings.
- **Tip tally** — a local count of the tips you have sent, shown so you can see
  your own support history. It is only a number on this device.

You can remove any of this by clearing the app's data or deleting its folder.

## What leaves the device

Ordinarily, nothing. There are two exceptions:

- **Store purchases.** In the App Store and Google Play builds, a Diamond
  subscription or a tip is checked by Apple or Google on the device. ezCORE
  only reads the result of that check to know what you have bought. The store
  handles the payment and your purchase details; ezCORE never receives them.
- **Update check on desktop download builds.** The Windows, Linux and macOS
  download builds make one request a day to the GitHub Releases API to see
  whether a newer version has been published. The request contains no
  information about you, your library or your saves. You can turn it off in
  Settings › About under "Check for updates automatically". Store builds never
  make this request.

## What ezCORE does not do

- No analytics or usage measurement.
- No crash reporting.
- No advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking.
- No account, sign-in or profile.
- No collection of personal information, and no sale or sharing of it.

## Children

ezCORE does not collect data from anyone, including children. There is no
account to create and no information to submit.
