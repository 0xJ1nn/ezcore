# Trademark policy

This project is not affiliated with, sponsored, or endorsed by Nintendo,
Sony, Sega, Atari, or any game publisher. All console and game titles
belong to their owners.

Rules for contributors and the app itself:

- Never use console brand names or logos in the app name, icon, or
  marketing copy. Describe compatibility functionally
  ("GB-compatible core", "plays your GBA dumps").
- Never bundle game art, box scans, or trademarked screenshots. Store
  listings show homebrew titles only.
- System labels in the UI use neutral short codes (`gb`, `snes`, …);
  the human-readable mapping in-app avoids brand styling.
